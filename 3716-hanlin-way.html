The Home

Finished space for everyday life and entertaining.

Welcome to 3716 Hanlin Way, a well-maintained home with a comfortable layout. The main level connects a spacious living room with the kitchen and dining area. A private bedroom wing includes three bedrooms, a full bath, and a convenient half bath.

The finished lower level adds a recreation room with a gas fireplace, built-in shelving, accent lighting, and additional entertainment space. A full bathroom, laundry area, and flexible room offer options for guests, a home office, hobbies, or storage.
