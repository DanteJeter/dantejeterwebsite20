The Home

Comfortable one-level living in a peaceful country setting.

Welcome to 43133 Old Irondale Road in Wellsville, Ohio. Set on approximately five acres, this well-maintained ranch offers 1,654 square feet of convenient one-level living with three bedrooms and two baths.

The home features a functional floor plan with spacious living areas, custom cabinetry, central air, and a fireplace. The detached garage supports parking, storage, hobbies, or additional workspace.

Outside, the property offers a private yard, mature trees, and views across the surrounding hills and pasture. Mineral rights convey with the property.
